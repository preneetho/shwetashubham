We confirm that the information provided is correct and understand that:

  1. All maintenance dues, approved charges, fines and liabilities relating to the flat must be cleared before the Society issues an NOC or move-out gate pass.
  2. Move-out must be informed in advance and carried out only between 7:00 AM-6:00 PM. The shifting vehicle must use the designated East-side driveway area without inconveniencing residents.
  3. A tenant move-out gate pass requires owner confirmation. Any damage observed during shifting may be charged to the flat.
  4. A resale NOC confirms society-account clearance only and does not certify legal title, ownership, statutory approvals or the terms of the proposed transaction.
  5. The Society may hold or withdraw the NOC if information is incorrect, dues remain unpaid, or required clearance is incomplete.
