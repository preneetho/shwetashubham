We, the undersigned Owner and Tenant, confirm that:

  1. We have received or accessed, read and understood the current Society Guidelines and agree to follow the society by-laws, notices and approved rules.
  2. The Tenant, occupants, domestic staff, guests and authorised vendors will comply with society safety, security, amenity, cleanliness, parking and conduct requirements.
  3. The Owner will ensure that tenant details remain current. The Owner and/or Tenant accept responsibility for damage, fines and violation charges attributable to the flat.
  4. No commercial activity will be carried out from the flat. Carpentry or in-house work will be restricted to 9:00 AM-7:00 PM, and quiet hours after 10:00 PM will be observed.
  5. Move-in or move-out will be informed in advance, carried out only between 7:00 AM-6:00 PM, and completed subject to applicable charges, clearances and gate-pass requirements.
  6. Any change in occupancy, mobile number or emergency contact will be promptly informed to the Society Office.
